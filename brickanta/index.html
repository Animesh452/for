1 Why Brickanta

Brickanta's description of the work matches my role at Trinity Tree, a clinical psychology practice, where I co-built an LLM pipeline that publishes nothing until a second model has checked it against source content and a clinician has approved it. Most of the hard work came after launch, when real reviewers and email systems behaved in ways the design hadn't planned for.

Forward-deployed work is the part I want more of. On a case-tracking system there, asking the person who would use it how they worked changed more of the design than any technical decision. I expect tender documents and historical estimates to be messier than the free-text notes I've parsed so far, and that's the appeal.
